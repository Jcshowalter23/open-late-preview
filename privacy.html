Privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026. This policy covers the Open Late website (openlateapp.com) and the Open Late apps for iPhone and Android.

Open Late has no accounts and no ads, and we don't sell or rent your information. Here is everything the site and apps collect, and why.

Your location stays on your device

When you tap Near me, your phone or browser asks for permission. Your location is used on your device only, to sort places by distance. It isn't sent to us or saved. To find nearby places, the app may download the list for nearby counties, so our web host sees which county lists were downloaded, the same as when you pick a county yourself.

Saved on your device

Your saved places, your last county and city pick, your list or map view, your deal thumbs-up and visit counts, and (in the apps) any event reminders you set are stored on your device only. Clearing the site's data or deleting the app removes them.

Anonymous visit counts (GoatCounter)

We use GoatCounter to count visits. It uses no cookies. It records the page viewed and a few anonymous events (such as which county or filter was picked, opening the map, sending a form, or a thumbs-up on a deal), along with the referring site, your browser, operating system, language and screen width, and the country worked out from your IP address. Your IP address is not stored, nothing is saved in your browser, and we can't identify you from these counts.

Google Analytics (website)

The openlateapp.com website also uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives the page you visit, your browser and device type, and your approximate location from your IP address. We use it only for traffic reports, not for advertising. Google handles this data under its own policy: see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The iPhone and Android apps don't include Google Analytics.

Forms you send (FormSubmit)

The Suggest an update, Add a place and Claim your listing forms send what you type to us by email through FormSubmit. That can include the place's name, city and address, the update or details you write, and, if you choose to give them, your name, email, phone number and your role at the business. It also includes the page you sent it from. We use it only to check and update listings and to reply to you. Your email and phone number are never shown in the app.

Payments (Stripe and Ko-fi)

Businesses that buy a Featured listing pay through Stripe. Stripe handles the card details; we never see or store card numbers. Stripe shares the buyer's name, email and payment status with us so we can feature the right listing. Optional tips go through Ko-fi on its own site, under its own policy.

Map tiles

The map loads its images from OpenStreetMap, which, like any website, sees your IP address and the map area you view. Direction links open Google Maps, which has its own policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no ads and no ad tracking.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We don't link what you do in Open Late with data from other companies' apps or sites for advertising.

Age

Open Late lists bars and drink specials, and is meant for adults 18 and older (and 21 and older to drink in the US). It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep form emails and purchase records only as long as we need them to run Open Late and meet tax or legal rules. Email us to see, correct or delete anything you've sent us, and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
